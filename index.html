A.R.G.U.S.

Advanced Response and General User Support

An assistant that lives on my PC and opens the moment I log in. I say its name, it listens, and we talk. It remembers what I said, knows tomorrow's weather, and can open apps for me. The AI runs on my own machine, so the conversation stays there.

What it does

Hands-free, from the moment the PC turns on

Holds a real conversation

Say “Argus” once, then keep talking. It listens for your reply after every answer and keeps the whole conversation in mind until things go quiet for 20 minutes.

Argus, what should I wear tomorrow?

Morning brief on a double clap

Two claps and it reads the time, today's weather in Katy, what to bring, and my priorities for the day. A new clap cuts it off and starts over.

👏 👏

Runs the computer by voice

Opens any installed app by name, closes windows, searches Google or YouTube, changes the volume and locks the PC. Shutdown is deliberately off-limits.

Argus, open Spotify

Live dashboard

Real CPU, memory, disk and network readings from the PC, live logs, and a 3D nebula you can spin with the mouse.

Argus, system status

Build notes

Problems worth solving

It kept answering itself

Follow-up questions came back mangled. The log showed why: the microphone was hearing ARGUS through the speakers and gluing its last sentence onto my question. Now it flushes the mic when it finishes talking and ignores anything that matches what it just said.

A brain that was never connected

Answers stayed generic even with the AI installed. The log showed it was never reaching the model: Windows tried the wrong network address first and gave up after two seconds. One address change fixed it.

Safe by design

It can only run actions on an approved list. Vague requests like “close it” get a question back, and it refuses to shut down or restart the PC.
